# Travel and Expense Policy (evaluation fixture)

This document is a synthetic fixture for the golden-set evaluation. Replace it with
real, non-sensitive company documents and update golden_set.jsonl to match.

## Booking
Employees must book flights through the corporate travel portal at least 14 days before
departure. Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is
allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer.

## Per diem
The daily meal allowance is 60 USD for domestic travel and 85 USD for international travel.
Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Expense claims
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of returning from a trip, with itemised
receipts for any single expense above 25 USD. Claims are approved by the employee's
line manager and paid with the next monthly payroll.
