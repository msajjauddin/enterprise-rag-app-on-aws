# Leave Policy (evaluation fixture)

This document is a synthetic fixture for the golden-set evaluation.

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued
monthly. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year and must be used
by 31 March.

## Sick leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required
for absences longer than 2 consecutive working days.

## Parental leave
Primary caregivers receive 26 weeks of paid parental leave; secondary caregivers
receive 4 weeks. Requests should be raised with HR at least 8 weeks in advance.
